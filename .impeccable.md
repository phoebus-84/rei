## Design Context

### Users

Property sellers and buyers in Ivrea, Canavese, and greater Piedmont (Italy). Typical contexts: homeowners looking to sell at fair value, first-time buyers seeking guidance, families searching rentals. They interact during high-stakes financial decisions — trust and clarity are paramount. Age range skews 30–60, digitally literate but not tech-savvy.

### Brand Personality

**Direct, professional, confident.** REI ("Real Estate Invest") positions itself as a young, transparent, numbers-driven agency. The copy voice is punchy and honest ("Non svendere, massimizza"). No corporate stuffiness, no hype — straightforward competence.

### Aesthetic Direction

- **Visual tone**: Warm Mediterranean professionalism. Grounded, trustworthy, locally rooted.
- **Theme**: Light mode primary. Warm whites and earth tones, not clinical.
- **Typography**: Bodoni Moda (display — Italian heritage serif with high-contrast strokes) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body — clean geometric sans). Fluid type scale for headings via `clamp()`, fixed `rem` for body and UI text.
- **Logo-derived palette**:
  - Teal (house outlines): primary — trust, expertise
  - Terracotta/orange (roof, "REI" text): accent — warmth, action, confidence
  - Amber (arrow): supporting highlight — energy, optimism
- **Anti-references**: AI-generated SaaS templates, neon gradients, dark-mode-with-glow, tech startup aesthetics, glassmorphism
- **References**: Italian editorial design, Piedmontese warmth, architectural photography sites

### Design Principles

1. **Earn trust through restraint** — No decorative gradients, no animated blobs. Every visual element should build credibility for a company handling major financial transactions.
2. **Territory first** — The design should feel rooted in Ivrea and the Canavese. Colors from the landscape (earth, stone, terracotta), not from a tech palette.
3. **Words do the work** — The copy is strong. Let typography and spacing elevate it rather than competing with decorative elements.
4. **One clear action** — Every section should guide toward a single next step. Don't overwhelm with options.
5. **Honest and complete** — No placeholder data, no dead links, no broken buttons. Everything the user sees should work.
