# Image Generation Guide for Homepage Sections

## Style Notes

- **Black & white only**, clean line art (no fills, no grayscale shading)
- **Consistent character**: bearded man, glasses, short tousled hair, button-up shirt
- **White background**, no border
- **Square aspect ratio** (~1:1)
- Objects/symbols should be simple and iconic (no excessive detail)
- Same line weight/thickness as existing illustrations
